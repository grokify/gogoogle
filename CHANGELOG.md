## [v0.13.0] - 2026-10-30

### Highlights

- Drive upload with conversion to Docs, Sheets, or Slides, and update in place
- Comments with quoted text, resolved state, and replies
- Suggested edits split into original, inserted, and deleted text
- Sheets read as header-keyed records, and CSV published as a Sheet
- `gogoogle drive`, `docs suggestions`, and `sheets records` commands

### Added

- `driveutil/v3`: Drive service with `GetFile` and the `ErrNotFound`, `ErrPermissionDenied`, and `ErrUnsupportedConversion` errors ([`9161f95`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/9161f95))
- `driveutil/v3`: `Upload` and `Update` with conversion to Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides ([`132fbb9`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/132fbb9))
- `driveutil/v3`: `ListComments` with quoted text, resolved state, replies, and complete paging ([`ed53951`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/ed53951))
- `docsutil/v1`: `ExtractSuggestionContent` classifies text as original, insertion, or deletion ([`aeec7f5`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/aeec7f5))
- `sheetsutil/v4`: `NewService` and `GetSpreadsheet` wrap the Sheets API service ([`6a6af52`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/6a6af52))
- `sheetsutil/v4`: `GetValues` and `BatchGetValues` read A1 ranges through the Sheets service ([`2a8746c`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/2a8746c))
- `slidesutil/v1`: `GetPresentation` on the Slides service ([`464c122`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/464c122))
- `sheetsutil/v4`: `ReadRecords` reads a sheet keyed by its header row, and `CreateFromCSV` publishes a CSV as a Sheet ([`adae614`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/adae614))
- `gogoogle drive upload`, `drive update`, `drive comments`, `docs suggestions`, and `sheets records` commands ([`c66fac7`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/c66fac7))

### Dependencies

- _4 dependency updates_

### Documentation

- Add guides for Drive upload and comments, Docs suggestions, Sheets records, and the new CLI commands ([`93ea382`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/93ea382))
- Add the PRD, TRD, plan, and roadmap for document review round-trip support ([`40aaf03`](https://github.com/grokify/gogoogle/commit/40aaf03))

### Tests
